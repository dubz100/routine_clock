Chosen · A

Morning Path

Big NOW with a shrinking disc, small NEXT, a proportional path along the top, a shared play jar and one lane of step bubbles per child.

  • Keeps the two best early-years tools (Now/Next and a visual timer) as the biggest things on screen
  • Play jar makes slow mornings a visible result, not a parent's verdict
  • Calm. Readable from across the room.
  • Most elements of the three. Solved by flooding the screen with one colour per step.

Runner-up · B

Morning Train

Each step is a carriage whose length is its duration. The play carriage stretches or squashes with the morning's pace.

  • Children love trains. Length = time is intuitive.
  • Current step is small and hard to read at 3 m
  • Overtime is awkward to show

Kept in A: proportional path segments and the squashing play segment.

Dropped · C

Rocket Countdown

A fuel tank fills as steps get done, and the rocket "launches" when you leave the house.

  • Very exciting finale
  • Doesn't say what to do right now
  • Two fuel lines invite "who's winning"
  • Over-stimulating first thing in the morning

Kept in A: the leaving celebration ("Door dance!" then "Bye bye house!").
